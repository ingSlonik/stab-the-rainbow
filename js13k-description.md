## 📖 Description

Legend has it that cosmic rainbows don't just stay glowing in space by good luck. They are maintained by celestial galloping unicorns equipped with razor-sharp cranial crystal horns!

In **Stab the Rainbow**, you gallop along an ethereal 7-lane rainbow suspended in the cosmos. But cosmic entropy is real: each color stripe under your hooves is constantly draining its energy, flickering in alarm, and dissolving into the dark astral abyss. If you step onto an empty void lane... unicorn flight capabilities fail immediately and you plunge into the infinite void!

To keep the road intact beneath your hooves, you must **aim and stab oncoming colored clouds with your forehead horn**. Impaling a cloud instantly recharges that color band back to 100% integrity with a brilliant celestial flash!

---

## 🎮 Controls

### 🥽 Virtual Reality (WebXR)
- **Aim Horn:** Move your **HEAD**. Look directly at approaching clouds to pierce them with your forehead horn!
- **Steer Across Lanes:** The unicorn naturally follows your horizontal gaze direction.
- **Jump Over Gaps:** Press any **VR Controller Trigger / Grip** (or tap screen) to leap gracefully over missing void lanes.

### 🖥️ Desktop (PC / Mac)
- **Aim Horn:** Move your **MOUSE** to direct your crystal horn and steer between rainbow lanes.
- **Micro-Steer:** **`A` / `D`** or **`←` / `→` Arrow Keys** to nudge lanes.
- **Jump Over Gaps:** Press **`SPACEBAR`** or **Left Click** to leap across void gaps.

- **WebXR & Three.js r185 (ESM):** Seamlessly runs in VR headsets (Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro, SteamVR) and standard desktop browsers with a unified 3D in-game canvas UI that works identically in all modes.
- **Generative Ezo-Ambient Audio:** Zero audio samples! 100% real-time procedural Web Audio synthesis featuring a soothing Lydian root drone, plus **7 spatial 3D chords** that dynamically pan left/right and swell in volume as colored clouds float toward your horn.
- **Tiny 8.3 KB ZIP Payload:** Handcrafted modular TypeScript bundled with esbuild, minified via Terser, crushed with Roadroller, and archived with ECT (`dist/stab-the-rainbow.zip` is **8,379 bytes**, well under the 13,312 byte limit).
